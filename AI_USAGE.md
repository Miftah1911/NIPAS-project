## 4. What the Team Did Independently

Beyond reviewing AI output, the team independently:

- **Defined the problem statement** — chosen from the specific challenges facing smallholder vegetable farmers in Bangladesh
- **Selected Track C (Agritech)** based on the fit between the project and the track's sustainability goals
- **Specified the bill of materials** — every component was chosen based on availability at Bangladeshi electronics vendors and cost constraints under ৳4,000
- **Designed the two-tier pricing model** — demo prototype vs commercial mid-tier, with realistic manufacturing cost breakdown
- **Selected MQTT over alternatives** (HTTP, raw WebSocket, LoRaWAN) after comparing bandwidth, cost, and reliability for rural Bangladeshi networks
- **Chose the offline-first architecture** — flash-backed buffering so the robot works without any network in the field
- **Tested the dashboard** on Chrome, Safari, and Firefox
- **Verified formula correctness** — spray duration calculation, buffer sizing, odometry conversion
- **Compiled every firmware sketch** in Arduino IDE 2.x and confirmed no errors
- **Cross-checked pin assignments** against the ESP32 datasheet and the L298N motor driver datasheet
- **Drafted the presentation narrative** — the story arc from problem to solution to roadmap
- **Practiced the live pitch** several times

## 5. What the Team Understands

Every algorithm and mechanism in this codebase is understood by the team. We can explain and modify each of the following without AI assistance:

### Firmware (ESP32 / ESP32-CAM)

- **State machine architecture** — why 8 states, how transitions are triggered, why some states are timed and others are condition-driven
- **Odometry calculation** — how encoder ticks convert to centimeters, why the wheel diameter matters, how calibration works
- **MQTT publish/subscribe model** — QoS levels, Last Will & Testament, topic namespacing for multi-robot scaling
- **Offline-first buffering** — how the ESP32 flash (Preferences library) stores telemetry, circular buffer design, auto-flush on reconnect
- **HSV color detection** — RGB to HSV conversion math, why hue is more robust than raw RGB for disease detection, how pixel-count ratios determine severity
- **UART protocol between ESP32 and ESP32-CAM** — why UART over WiFi direct, JSON framing, timeout handling
- **PWM motor control via L298N** — why H-bridge architecture, how to reverse direction, speed control
- **Interrupt-driven encoder counting** — why ISRs are used, how to avoid missing ticks

### Dashboard (HTML / CSS / JS)

- **Chart.js integration** — how the telemetry charts stream data, dual-axis scaling
- **State management** — how the JS `state` object tracks all data and drives UI updates
- **MQTT.js over WebSocket** — how browser subscribes to topics, reconnection logic
- **KPI card pulse animations** — CSS keyframes and how they trigger on data change
- **Toast notification system** — dynamic DOM insertion and cleanup
- **Settings persistence** — what would need to be added for localStorage

### System-Level

- **Why MQTT over HTTP** for this use case — bandwidth, cost, QoS, persistent connections
- **Why offline-first matters in rural Bangladesh** — network reliability, cost, farmer behavior
- **Bill of materials choices** — why 37GB metal-gear motors over TT plastic, why JSN-SR04T over HC-SR04
- **Cost structure** — manufacturing cost vs retail price, margin calculations
- **Track C alignment** — how NIPAS maps to the rulebook's requirements for resource conservation, offline-first, and MQTT scaling
