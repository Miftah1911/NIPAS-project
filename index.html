The Problem

Smallholder farmers lose income and water every season

Five specific, measurable problems affect millions of families in rural Bangladesh.

70%
Water wasted by flood irrigation
30–40%
Farm income spent on chemicals
60–70%
Chemical lost to runoff
3–5
Days between invisible and unrecoverable disease

Add to that aging farmers — average age over 50 — and there simply aren't enough hands to walk every field.

Our Solution

An autonomous robot that sees disease and sprays only what needs it

NIPAS drives crop rows autonomously, scans every plant with on-device HSV vision, and delivers targeted micro-doses of water or fungicide.

📷

On-device vision

HSV color analysis detects chlorosis and necrosis before symptoms are visible. No cloud, no GPU.

💧

Targeted misting

Water and fungicide delivered only to the plant that needs it. Dose scales with severity.

📡

MQTT telemetry

Live dashboard over MQTT — 100-byte packets, works over 2G/3G/4G. Standard protocol for sensor networks.

📴

Offline-first

Robot works with zero network. Buffers 45 days of data to flash. Syncs when connectivity returns.

How It Works

Autonomous state machine · on-device HSV vision

8-state machine — every transition is published to the dashboard in real time.

⏸️ IDLE

Waiting for START

➡️ MOVING

Watching encoders + ultrasonic

📷 SCANNING

CAM sweep + HSV analysis

💦 SPRAYING

Dose ∝ severity

Plus OBSTACLE_AVOID, TURNING, ROW_COMPLETE, FINISHED

HSV detection ranges — tunable per crop, tuned for tomato / chili / brinjal.

💚 Healthy leaf H 80–150
💛 Chlorosis H 40–75
🤎 Necrosis H 10–40

Infection ratio determines spray dose: <5% healthy · 5–15% low · 15–30% moderate · >30% high

Honest Assessment

What we have not solved yet — and what we have

✅ Complete

Software & Design

  • Full autonomous firmware (state machine + offline buffer)
  • ESP32-CAM HSV vision pipeline
  • MQTT telemetry with Last Will & Testament
  • Operator dashboard — 8 KPIs, 2 charts, audit log
  • Full BOM, wiring diagrams, calibration tools
⏳ Planned

Current Limitations

  • Hardware not yet assembled — design complete, build phase next
  • Timed 180° turns (gyro upgrade planned)
  • HSV is sunlight-sensitive — auto-calibration planned
  • No weed vs crop distinction yet (TinyML on roadmap)
  • 2–3 hr battery per charge — solar on roadmap

We believe a real engineering project tells you where it stops working — not just where it shines.
